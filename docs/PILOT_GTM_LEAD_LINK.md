# Builder pilot GTM — lead link first (no WhatsApp trial blasts)

Ramatech sells a **qualification layer**: Google Ads → LeadPilot chat → HOT/WARM caller queue.

**Do not** pitch brand WhatsApp for the 30-day pilot. Trial WhatsApp shows “Twilio Trial” and template sample text.

---

## Target

3–5 builders already known to Ramatech, with live Google Ads (≈ ₹2–15L/mo).

## Pitch (one paragraph)

> LeadPilot scores every Ads enquiry over chat so your call centre only dials HOT and WARM. 30-day pilot, no subscription. You paste one link into Ads. WhatsApp Business branding comes later after Meta approval.

## Builder setup (15 min)

1. Sign up on the live LeadPilot URL.
2. Add one project.
3. Copy **lead link** → Google Ads final URL / landing CTA.
4. Call team: Leads → **Call now (HOT+WARM)** each morning.
5. Skip Channels → WhatsApp unless Ramatech sets production WABA.

## Weekly review questions

1. Ads clicks vs chats started?
2. HOT / WARM / COLD mix (if ~80% HOT, scoring is wrong)?
3. Are callers skipping COLD?
4. “Would you pay ₹X/month?” — write the number down.

## Anti-patterns

- Importing 100 fake CSV numbers and blasting openers
- Expecting friends to get messages without Twilio verified recipients
- Promising “Rays Builders” on WhatsApp while on Twilio trial

## Success gate → subscription work

Only start Razorpay / plans when **≥2 pilots** say they would keep paying without Ramatech sitting on every call.
