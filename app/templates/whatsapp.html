What you need

🧪 For testing (today, free)

Twilio trial WhatsApp is not the old sandbox free-text send. You must: (1) verify each friend’s number in Try out WhatsApp (max 5), (2) paste the trial ContentSid (HX…) above, (3) use the From number shown on that same page. Join-code alone is not enough on a new trial account.

🏢 For production (real leads)

A verified WhatsApp Business number. You'll need:

  • A phone number not active on regular WhatsApp
  • A Meta Business Manager account
  • Business verification — registration certificate & address proof
  • An approved display name (your agency name)
  • An approved message template for the first message
⏱️
Meta's review usually takes 1–3 days. Until it's approved, your web lead link keeps working normally — leads still get qualified.

The 24-hour rule

WhatsApp only allows free-form messages within 24 hours of a lead's last reply. Outside that window the first message must be a pre-approved template. Your AI agent's replies during a live conversation are always inside this window.
